Furnace Making Noise in Chicago? What to Notice

A furnace can make normal airflow and startup sounds, but a new banging, rattling, squealing or grinding sound deserves attention. Note whether the sound happens at startup, while the blower is running, or when the furnace shuts down.

Do not ignore worsening sounds

Turn the system off and seek professional help if a sound is severe, accompanied by a burning smell, smoke, or another safety concern.
