About FurnaceCheckNow

FurnaceCheckNow is a simple starting point for Chicago-area homeowners who need help with a furnace problem, inspection or maintenance request.

We focus on making the first step easy: describe the problem, provide your contact information and ZIP code, and we can follow up about the request.

FurnaceCheckNow is not presented as a manufacturer, licensing body, review organization or emergency service.
