About Chicago Furnace Check

Chicago Furnace Check was built to make the first step simpler when a furnace is not acting normally.

Instead of pretending every furnace symptom means the same thing, the site organizes common homeowner questions—no heat, cold air, noises, maintenance and replacement—and provides a simple request form when the basic checks do not solve the problem.

Chicago Furnace Check is a lead-intake and information website. It does not represent itself as the technician performing every service request.
