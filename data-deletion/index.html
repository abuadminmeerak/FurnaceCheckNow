Data Deletion Request

You may request deletion of personal information you have submitted to FurnaceCheckNow.com.

How to Request Deletion

Email support@furnacechecknow.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request. Include the name, phone number, or email address you used when contacting us so we can identify the information associated with your request.

We may need to verify your request before deleting information. Some records may be retained when reasonably necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or recordkeeping obligations.
