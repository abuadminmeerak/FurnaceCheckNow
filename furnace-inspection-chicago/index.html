Chicago Furnace Inspection: What Homeowners Should Expect

A seasonal furnace check generally focuses on safe operation, heating performance, airflow and visible maintenance needs. The exact work depends on the equipment and the service provider.

When to schedule

Homeowners often choose to have heating equipment checked before heavy winter use or when they notice a change in comfort, sound, cycling or performance.
